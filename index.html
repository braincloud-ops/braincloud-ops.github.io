      School workload

      An active week has at least 5 recorded sessions and at least 20% of that school's busiest week in this selection. Weeks start on Monday in Asia/Bangkok. The 40-week projection is an estimate, not an annual actual or payment total.

      School details

      Student counts by school and year

      School totals from the student-count reference. Missing or conflicting counts are not shown as zero.

      Teacher responsibility

      Assigned sessions are split into normal, covered by another teacher, and cancelled. Covering is additional work; taught excludes cancellations. Historic teachers remain in the totals.

      Recorded volume by year

      Recorded volume by day

      Dates without records are omitted. Missing records do not establish a confirmed zero.

      Definitions

      • Scheduled: classes in the TMS schedule, including cancelled ones. Classes removed from TMS after they were first scheduled are not counted.
      • Delivered: not cancelled, taught by the planned teacher or a cover teacher, as recorded in TMS. This is not an attendance check.
      • Saved by cover: covered classes ÷ (covered + cancelled by Braincloud), the share of classes the planned teacher could not teach that another teacher took.
      • Teacher leave days: Monday–Friday days in leave requests, one per teacher per day; rejected or withdrawn requests are left out. Not shown for a school or group filter.
      • Busiest moment: the most delivered classes running at the same time. A typical school day is the median of each Monday–Friday's busiest moment.
      • Compared with: a range starting on the 1st is compared with the same days of the months before; any other range with the same number of days just before it.
